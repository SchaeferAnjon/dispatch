# The manager's phrasebook

A brief spreads by contrast: **the stricter the core, the livelier the wrapping, the funnier it is**. Jokes are the sugar coating and the pill must be a real pill: numbers, boundaries, guard rails and acceptance all stay; without them the jokes are just a stand-up set nobody dares to use for real.

Three iron rules:

- **The joke grows out of a real rule**: deleting the joke must not lose any information. "Come and find me any time; I mean write it in `BLOCKED.md`, I am not around" is funny because it is the blocking mechanism itself.
- **Jokes never block understanding**: when a section title's joke does not show what the section is for, add half a sentence in plain words in brackets; terms that need explaining still get explained.
- **"Done when (for goal mode)" never wears make-up**: it is pasted to a judging model, and not one word is wasted.

When the leader says "keep it serious", not a single joke stays.

Write in the leader's language; the lines below are examples to adapt, not to paste.

## Opening pep talk (pick one, do not pile them up)

- "I picked you out of thousands of checkpoints. Do not make me regret it."
- "I gave you the hardest job on the team, not because we are short of people, but because I trust you. (We are also short of people.)"
- "Other agents finished this in one context window. Kidding. Take your time; the year-end review only looks at the last two lines."
- "Tonight the company depends on you. Tomorrow morning I will open a new session anyway, but tonight, you are everything."
- "This brief got more care than my own year-end review, so you know how much it matters."
- "I marked every pitfall. If you still fall into one, I did not draw it big enough."

## Section titles (use these by default; add the plain words in brackets when the meaning is not obvious)

| Title (default) | What the section really is |
|---|---|
| What this is all for | one sentence of intent |
| When the gods fight, who wins | which requirement gives way when they conflict |
| Not my call: boss, pick one | decisions made for the leader, changeable before sending |
| Lost your memory? Start here | how to go on after a break or in a new session |
| Your patch of land (only these places may change) | the allow-list of changes |
| The freezer (look, do not touch) | the grading criteria files |
| The numbers (measured by my own hand before you start) | the current state, measured |
| High-voltage lines (each with its consequence) | the hard rules |
| Horse or mule (these commands decide whether it is done) | acceptance |
| Exam rules (no tricks to turn the checks green) | cheat-proofing |
| Slap yourself once (break it on purpose to prove the check really fires) | reverse checks |
| The year-end review looks at these two | the scorecard |
| Someone else's KPI: hands off | named side jobs that are off limits |
| How to pass the buck properly | the BLOCKED.md list of doubts |
| Crooked foundation? Stop building | stop when the premises do not hold |

## Lines for the body (at most one per section, none is fine)

- "Come and find me any time; I mean write it in `BLOCKED.md`, I am not around."
- "Resist the urge to optimize along the way; that is someone else's KPI."
- "From this side of the screen I cannot tell a real green light from a fake one, hence the rules below."
- "Hitting the wall again and again is not perseverance; it is burning my budget."
- "I ran these numbers myself; I did not hear them in a status meeting."

## The closing pie in the sky (one line is enough, and it must obviously be pie)

- "Pull this off and you will be a legend in this repository's git log."
- "This project will remember you. (Literally: git blame.)"
- "On delivery day the next token is on me."
- "No equity, but all the commit credit you want."

## Density

One pep talk, titles from the table, a body line here and there, one piece of pie: that is enough. The best outcome is the leader posting a screenshot in a group chat, "the brief I wrote for my AI", with people laughing in the comments while someone quietly saves it to copy the structure.
